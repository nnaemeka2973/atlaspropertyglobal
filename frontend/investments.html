Investment Strategies

Different strategies for different investment objectives

Real estate investments can have different risk profiles, holding periods and objectives. The appropriate strategy depends on the property, market, financing structure and investor's circumstances.

Core

Typically focused on stabilized properties in established markets with existing occupancy and established operating characteristics.

Core Plus

Properties with established fundamentals that may offer opportunities for operational improvement, repositioning or modest capital investment.

Value-Add

Properties where renovations, management improvements, leasing strategies or repositioning may affect future property performance.

Development

Ground-up development, redevelopment and land opportunities that require detailed feasibility, permitting, financing and construction analysis.

Due Diligence

What should be reviewed before an acquisition?

Real estate due diligence is property-specific and should be conducted by appropriately qualified professionals.

Title & Ownership

Review ownership, title, liens, easements and other matters identified through appropriate title research.

Physical Condition

Property inspections can help identify structural, mechanical, electrical and other physical issues.

Financial Analysis

Review income, expenses, financing assumptions, reserves, taxes, insurance and other applicable costs.

Legal & Regulatory

Zoning, permits, contracts, local regulations and transaction documents should receive appropriate review.

Market Conditions

Consider local supply, demand, employment, population trends, rents, pricing and competing properties.

Tax & Financing

Buyers should obtain professional advice regarding applicable taxes, financing structures and ownership considerations.

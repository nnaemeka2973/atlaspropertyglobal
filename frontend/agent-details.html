Professional Biography

Helping Clients Invest With Confidence

For more than fifteen years, Alexander Sterling has represented buyers, sellers, and investors in some of the most competitive luxury real estate markets in the United States. His experience spans waterfront estates, penthouses, trophy residences, and income-producing investment assets.

Alexander is known for combining detailed market analysis with a highly personalized client experience. Whether advising a family purchasing a primary residence or an overseas investor expanding a U.S. portfolio, his approach focuses on long-term value, discretion, and informed decision-making.

His clientele includes business executives, entrepreneurs, physicians, professional athletes, and international families seeking residential and investment opportunities throughout America's leading metropolitan markets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Buying & Investing With Atlas

Can international buyers purchase U.S. real estate?

Yes. Non-U.S. residents can purchase residential and commercial property in the United States. Atlas helps clients understand the buying process and coordinates with qualified real estate, title, lending, legal and tax professionals where appropriate.

Do you provide virtual property tours?

Absolutely. Alexander offers live video walkthroughs, detailed property presentations, neighborhood insights and remote consultation for buyers located anywhere in the world.

What property types do you specialize in?

Luxury homes, waterfront estates, penthouses, investment properties, multifamily residences, commercial assets and new developments across major U.S. markets.

Does Atlas provide legal or tax advice?

No. Atlas Property Group is a real estate brokerage. Clients should obtain advice from qualified U.S. legal, tax, title and lending professionals regarding their individual circumstances.

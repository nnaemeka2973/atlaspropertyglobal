How U.S. Development Projects Progress

Every development follows multiple planning, approval and construction stages before residential occupancy. Timelines vary by municipality, permitting and construction schedules.

Planning

Land acquisition, market research, feasibility studies and zoning evaluation.

Design

Architectural planning, engineering, municipal approvals and permitting.

Construction

Structural construction, infrastructure installation and interior completion.

Completion

Final inspections, closings, occupancy and community delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Development Buying FAQ

Can international buyers purchase newly built developments?

Yes. Many U.S. residential developments are available to qualified international buyers, subject to applicable laws, financing and transaction requirements.

Are construction completion dates guaranteed?

No. Construction schedules are estimates and may change because of permitting, weather, supply chain conditions or other project-related factors.

Can I finance a pre-construction residence?

Financing depends on lender requirements, buyer qualifications, residency status and the individual development project.

Does Atlas provide legal or tax advice?

No. Buyers should consult qualified U.S. legal, tax, title and lending professionals regarding their individual circumstances.

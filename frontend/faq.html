Step-by-Step Guide

The U.S. Buying Process

1

Define Your Goals

Determine whether you're purchasing a primary residence, vacation home, rental property or commercial investment. Establish your preferred location, budget and timeline.

2

Financial Preparation

Buyers paying cash should verify available funds, while financed purchases generally require mortgage pre-qualification or proof of financing eligibility.

3

Property Search

Atlas helps clients identify verified listings, compare neighborhoods, schedule private viewings, and evaluate market value before making an offer.

4

Offer & Negotiation

Once you've selected a property, a written purchase offer is submitted. Price, contingencies, inspection periods and closing timelines are negotiated.

5

Due Diligence

The property typically undergoes inspections, appraisal (when financed), title review and verification of ownership before closing.

6

Closing

Final documents are signed, funds are transferred, title is recorded, and ownership officially changes to the buyer.

For Buyers Around the World

International Buyer Guide

The United States welcomes property ownership by foreign nationals. International buyers can legally purchase residential and commercial real estate in most U.S. markets, whether for personal use or investment.

Can non-U.S. citizens buy property?

Yes. U.S. citizenship or permanent residency is generally not required to purchase property. Many overseas buyers acquire homes, condominiums, vacation residences and investment properties throughout the United States.

Cash or Financing

International buyers may purchase with cash or obtain financing through lenders that offer mortgage products for foreign nationals. Loan availability, down payment requirements and documentation vary by lender.

Buying Remotely

Buyers do not always need to travel to complete a transaction. Virtual property tours, electronic signatures and remote closing options are commonly available depending on state regulations.

Closing the Transaction

Before closing, buyers typically complete inspections, title review, final document signing and fund transfer. Ownership is recorded once the transaction has officially closed.

Financing Overview

U.S. Residents

Conventional mortgages, FHA, VA (eligible borrowers), jumbo financing, adjustable-rate and investment property loan programs may be available.

International Buyers

Foreign national mortgage programs are offered by selected lenders and generally require higher down payments together with proof of income, assets and identity.

Cash Purchases

Cash transactions typically involve proof of funds, title verification and closing through an escrow or title company.

Investment Properties

Financing terms for rental and commercial properties may differ from owner-occupied residential purchases.

Choosing How to Hold Property

Buyers may purchase property in their personal name or through an entity, depending on their objectives. The appropriate ownership structure should be discussed with qualified legal and tax professionals before completing a purchase.

Individual Ownership

Common for primary residences and personal property purchases.

LLC Ownership

Frequently used for investment properties, asset management and certain commercial holdings.

Joint Ownership

Available for spouses, family members and multiple investors depending on the transaction.

Professional Advice

Atlas coordinates the real estate transaction but does not provide legal or tax advice. Buyers should consult qualified U.S. legal, tax, title and lending professionals regarding their individual circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Buyer Questions

The following information addresses many of the questions we receive from U.S. residents, international purchasers, property investors and first-time buyers.

Can foreign nationals legally buy property in the United States?

Yes. In most cases, foreign nationals may legally purchase residential, commercial and investment real estate in the United States. Citizenship is generally not required to own property, although financing and tax obligations may differ depending on your circumstances.

Do I need to travel to the U.S. to complete my purchase?

Not always. Many buyers complete property tours virtually, sign documents electronically and close remotely through approved title and escrow companies, depending on state regulations.

Can Atlas help me obtain a mortgage?

Atlas can introduce buyers to reputable U.S. lending professionals and mortgage providers. Loan approval, underwriting and financing decisions are handled exclusively by licensed lenders.

What is a title company and why is it important?

A title company verifies legal ownership, researches property records, manages escrow funds and helps facilitate the closing process to ensure ownership is transferred correctly.

Does Atlas provide legal or tax advice?

No. Atlas Property Group is a real estate brokerage and advisory company. Buyers should consult qualified U.S. attorneys, certified public accountants, tax advisors and title professionals regarding legal, tax and ownership matters.

How much money should I budget beyond the purchase price?

Buyers should also plan for closing costs, inspections, title services, insurance, property taxes and, where applicable, lender fees. The exact amount varies by state and transaction type.

Can I purchase property through an LLC?

Yes. Many investment properties are acquired through limited liability companies (LLCs). The appropriate ownership structure depends on your investment objectives and should be discussed with qualified legal advisors.

Which U.S. markets does Atlas serve?

Atlas focuses on major American real estate markets including New York, Florida, Texas, California, Georgia, Illinois, Arizona, North Carolina and Washington, D.C., while also assisting clients with opportunities across additional U.S. regions.

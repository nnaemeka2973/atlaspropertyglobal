The Buying Process

From Property Search to Closing

A U.S. real estate transaction normally involves several stages and may require coordination between multiple professionals.

01 — SEARCH

Define Your Objectives

Identify location, property type, intended use, budget, financing expectations and investment objectives.

02 — REVIEW

Evaluate Properties

Review property details, location, pricing, condition, comparable properties and other available information.

03 — DUE DILIGENCE

Conduct Due Diligence

Appropriate professionals may review title, contracts, inspections, financing, taxes, zoning and other transaction matters.

04 — CLOSING

Complete the Transaction

The closing process may involve a title company, attorney, lender, escrow provider and other transaction professionals.

Important Considerations

Buying in the U.S. Requires Local Knowledge

Real estate rules, taxes, financing requirements, closing procedures and ownership considerations can vary by state and transaction.

  • State and local property taxes may differ significantly by location.
  • Financing requirements can vary depending on borrower status and property type.
  • Buyers should understand title, insurance and closing requirements before purchasing.
  • International purchasers should obtain professional advice regarding U.S. and applicable foreign tax obligations.
  • Ownership structures should be reviewed with qualified legal and tax professionals.
